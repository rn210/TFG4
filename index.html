Daily Choices

This is a made-up story about a worker named Meena. She has a small amount of money for the week. Each day, you choose what Meena should do. See what happens after 5 days.

  • You learn to manage daily money and save cash.
  • You learn how to handle surprise bills.
  • Needs are things like food and a place to live.

Scenario 2

Corner Stall

This is a made-up story about a small food stall run by a seller named Raju. Each morning, choose how much food he should make. Food costs ₹5 to make. It sells for ₹12. Food that is not sold is wasted. The stall costs ₹30 each day to run. Play for 10 days and see how much money is left at the end.

  • You run your own small shop.
  • You make choices to keep your shop open.
  • Sales money helps you pay for rent and supplies.
  • Profit is the money you keep after you pay all bills.
  • You must keep extra cash to buy supplies for new sales.

Scenario 4

The Loan Challenge

You have a small business and a family to look after. Decide when borrowing helps, how much to borrow, and how to repay what you owe. Play 4 rounds and see the real cost of each loan.

  • A loan gives you money now, but you must return it later.
  • Interest is the extra money you pay for borrowing.
  • Borrow only what you need and can repay.
  • A smaller payment today can become a bigger debt tomorrow.
  • Keeping some cash for emergencies makes repayment safer.
